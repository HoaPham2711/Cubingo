Your content stays on your device

Cubingo is built to work on your device first. What you create in the app is stored on your device and is not uploaded to us. It only leaves your device when you choose to share or export it, and then only to the place you pick.

Usage analytics and crash reports

To understand which features are used and to fix problems, the app uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. They receive app usage events (for example, which screens are opened), an app-specific installation identifier, the device model, the operating system and app versions, and, when the app crashes, a crash report. This information is not linked to your name or e-mail address, and we do not use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

You can turn off usage analytics at any time in the app's settings ("Share usage data").

We do not sell your data
